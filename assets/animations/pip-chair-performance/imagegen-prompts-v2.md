# Chair pose ImageGen prompts

ImageGen built-in mode was used with transparent output. References were assigned explicitly: the approved seated Pip sprite for identity and scale, the room illustration for chair perspective, and preceding generated chair poses for acting continuity.

## Contact pose

Draw Pip at the instant his hips contact the chair: knees fold, belly and lower body compress slightly, head dips, flippers catch balance, scarf follows upward, and feet hang below the seat with toes angled down. Preserve the same head size and sprite margins; do not enlarge or stretch Pip.

## Settle pose

Draw the follow-through between contact and rest: hips remain supported, feet continue to dangle, torso rises slightly, flippers return halfway toward the thighs, head lifts, and scarf falls toward its relaxed drape. Preserve exact scale and character identity.

## Rest pose

Edit the existing seated key pose only: keep Pip's face, head, scarf, outlines, colors, and canvas placement fixed; bend the knees downward and let both feet hang just below the implied seat edge. Keep the original alpha bounds and character size.
