It retargets cold, wave, hopeful-wave, scarf-tuck, curious-look, and celebrate JSONs. `build-blink.mjs` produces a dedicated blink check. Only cold, blink and wave have been exported to MP4 in this review; other retargeted JSONs are not visually approved.

No obvious duplicate limbs, exposed shoulder gaps, or scarf-side changes were seen in those samples. The cold movement is intentionally restrained. The blink is an independently authored painted overlay, not full-character frames.

Character identity acceptance remains the user's visual decision.
