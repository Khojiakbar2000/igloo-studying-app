## Master prompt, final refinement pass

Refine the provided IGLOO interior master painting into a new composition; keep its beautiful hand-painted snow and wood finish, cool blue-violet ice shadows, warm lantern color, and exact room character, but correct the perspective and spatial design decisively.

The current view is too frontal. Rotate the camera to a clearly oblique three-quarter view from just inside the room's front-right edge, looking diagonally toward the far-left/back wall. The room should feel like an enclosed curved cave one can physically stand inside, with the domed ceiling visibly receding overhead into the far wall, curved side walls wrapping back, and a large floor plane widening toward the camera. Avoid a symmetrical front elevation, flat dollhouse stage, outside igloo shell framing, or orthographic/isometric game map. Use strong but natural perspective convergence: a rug with an oval footprint, desk sides at different apparent angles, shelf receding along the wall, and visible depth on the bed.

Design a clear navigation-first layout: keep the bed compact and against the left wall in the mid-ground; small round window on the far wall; compact bookshelf fitted to the rear-right curved wall; desk against the right wall in the mid-ground; chair pulled away from desk toward the central floor, with an obvious gap so the small penguin can approach, walk around, and climb up; lantern on the desk. Leave the central and near floor as a broad, uncluttered walk lane connecting a narrow arched doorway on the back-left wall to the chair, window, bed, and shelf. Keep all furnishings compact, footprints grounded and visually separated.

Keep the welcoming painted-book quality and handcrafted details but reduce decoration. Snow blocks must vary organically and follow the room's curvature. Blue environmental shadow, soft lantern pool, cool far wall, brighter near floor, consistent upper-left cool fill. Preserve subtle brush texture. The room interior fills the image; window shows only quiet twilight, no landscape vignette outside. No penguin in this architecture-only master, no UI, text, extra plants, excessive decor, symmetrical layout, floating furniture, wrong perspective, thick outlines, clean vector shapes, photorealism, or glossy 3D.

References used by role: Study 2 for arrangement only; Study 1 for warmth and painted treatment; Study 3 for ceiling curvature and soft light. Existing penguin was kept out of the architecture plate so the character can remain a separately controlled scene asset.

## Editable-source boundary

The master paintings are flattened raster outputs. The editable source preserved for this stage is this prompt/decision record plus the vector navigation/layer plan; neither PNG is a separated furniture or paint layer file. Production layer separation is intentionally deferred until after the occlusion prototype proves the split-object approach. Repainted, animation-ready layers will need hand correction around every cut edge; automatic extraction from the master is not approved as final artwork.
