# Igloo ambient audio

The following field recordings are by Joseph Sardin / BigSoundBank and are released under CC0. The app includes trimmed excerpts with overlapped loop seams, gain matching, and a 24 kHz PCM export. The heavy rain excerpt also has gentle peak compression.

| Igloo sound | Recording |
| --- | --- |
| Rain | [Rain Under an Umbrella](https://bigsoundbank.com/rain-under-an-umbrella-s2679.html) |
| Heavy Rain | [Rain and Thunder #3](https://bigsoundbank.com/storm-and-rain-3-s2717.html) |
| Ocean | [Beach: Small Waves](https://bigsoundbank.com/beach-small-waves-s0265.html) |
| Forest | [Forest](https://bigsoundbank.com/forest-s0100.html) |
| Fireplace | [Fireplace #2](https://bigsoundbank.com/fireplace-2-s0031.html) |
| Snowstorm | [Whistling of the Wind #1](https://bigsoundbank.com/whistling-of-the-wind-1-s0147.html) |
| Night | [Campaign at Night #4](https://bigsoundbank.com/campaign-at-night-4-s1880.html) |

White, brown, and pink noise were generated for Igloo using FFmpeg's colored noise source and share the same PCM loop preparation. They contain no third-party recordings.
